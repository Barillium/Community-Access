## Description
Community Access combines event discovery with personal access to existing communities.

Explore publicly shared individual events and organizers. Find information about admission, accessibility and on-site awareness support.

Protected communities and events have their own access requirements. An invitation lets you apply; the assigned community moderators decide on admission. Community membership, event confirmation and ticket purchases remain separate steps. Protected venue details are shown only with the required current authorization.

When an organizer offers tickets, you can review the offer and seller details, pay and follow your order status. The organizer is your contractual ticket seller.

Hosts manage their own profiles, events, applications and invitations. Shared management is explicitly assigned and can be revoked.

You can browse public information without an account. Personal features require an account. The intended minimum account age is 16; events may have additional age and admission requirements.

Community Access complements existing contact channels. For help, content reports or privacy requests, contact the team through the app.
